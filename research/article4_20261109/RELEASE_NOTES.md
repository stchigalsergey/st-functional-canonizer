Version 0.4.0 publishes the computational companion to the revised Article 4:
“Stability Analysis of Category Profiles for Structured Text Code Differences Across All Optimal Alignments.”

Download **Article4_reproducibility_2026-11-09.zip**, extract it, and follow README.txt.
The ZIP, rather than the automatically generated repository source archive, contains the complete prepared experiment directory.

Results and contents:

- 675 exhaustive checks of exact category-count bounds, including occurrence-dependent categories.
- All-optimal-path graphs, six tie-breaking orders, and witness paths.
- Synthetic results: 188 pairs, 34 with multiple optimal paths, six with variable category counts.
- Raw AutoPLC pilot: 11 of 12 pairs represented, eight with multiple paths, seven with variable profiles.
- A separate declared terminal-fence normalization yields 12 represented pairs, nine ambiguous alignments, and eight variable profiles.
- A matched single-output comparison on 174 non-permutation cases; 14 permutations explicitly excluded.
- Nine information-loss controls, 6,415 traced occurrences, and 591 source-linked edit operations.
- Original AI-preannotated forms, subsequent specialist-checked forms, and agreed decisions are stored separately. Derived static arguments are not new independent expert annotations.
- Source provenance, notices, input/output hashes, and five reproduction commands.

The archive was reproduced locally in a separate unpacked directory. The publication workflow independently reruns its computations on a hosted runner as an automated check. Neither check is independent third-party scientific validation.

This release does not claim ST compilation, PLC execution, behavioral equivalence of the full corpus, engineering classifier accuracy, model ranking, or reduced engineer diagnosis time. Earlier v0.3.0 results remain historical inputs and are not counted as an independent new dataset.
